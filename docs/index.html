CTR Opportunity Scoring for Search Content Review

A leakage-aware, human-reviewed ranking system for finding visible pages that may be under-capturing clicks.

Feature window: March 2026Outcome window: April 2026Decision metric: Precision@10
Abstract

Can search visibility signals prioritize pages that deserve metadata or content review?

I used the pseudonymized FlyRank internship warehouse, aggregated available Search Console observations to client-content-month rows, and compared a transparent CTR-gap baseline with Logistic Regression. On a client-grouped April outcome split, the baseline measured Precision@10 of 0.40 and Logistic Regression measured 0.30. The result is directional decision-support evidence: the simpler baseline is preferable for this slice, and no content change should be automated from the score.

01 · Problem

A queue, not a publishing bot

The decision is which visible pages an SEO or content reviewer should inspect first. The output is an ordered queue with one reason code and one action label. A wrong recommendation costs review time or may prompt an unnecessary content change, so the system deliberately stops before publishing.

03 · Method

Transparent before complex

The baseline score is positive CTR gap versus a position benchmark multiplied by log1p(impressions). The model is Logistic Regression over impressions, CTR, average position, organic sessions, and CTR gap. Validation keeps all rows from a client in one split.

  1. Check CTR-vs-position and impression-volume signals in buckets.
  2. Rank by the baseline score and assign CTR_UNDERCAPTURE.
  3. Compare the baseline and model on the same held-out clients.
  4. Audit future-window leakage and inspect false positives.

The baseline measured higher Precision@10 on held-out clients. That is an observed comparison on one March-to-April slice, not evidence that the rule causes future improvement. Model errors include false positives caused by SERP context, position, seasonality, intent mismatch, and incomplete engagement coverage.

05 · Limits

What the score cannot know

  • A CTR gap does not prove that weak metadata or content caused under-capture.
  • The panel is unbalanced, GA4 coverage is incomplete, and one outcome month cannot establish stability.
  • Hash keys are pseudonymous and do not reveal client or page identity.
  • Human review is mandatory before any metadata or content change.
06 · Actions

Recommended review order

  1. Inspect the highest baseline-scoring pages for title, description, SERP features, and intent alignment.
  2. Reject recommendations where position, seasonality, or page intent explains the gap.
  3. Measure future Precision@10 across additional months before replacing the baseline.
  4. Do not automate publishing, deletion, redirects, or causal claims.
